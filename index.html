Opportunity Tracking Analysis Dashboard

This dashboard delivers strategic insights into sales pipeline performance and opportunity management. It showcases total revenue ($2.11B), weighted revenue ($461.12M), sales velocity ($1.01M), and total opportunities (486). The analysis breaks down revenue by region and sales channel (Direct vs. Partner), evaluates average sales cycle duration, conversion rates, and opportunity distribution. Designed for sales leadership, this dashboard supports data-driven decisions to optimize channel performance and improve revenue forecasting accuracy.

KPMG Sprocket Analysis Dashboard

This dashboard provides a comprehensive performance overview of KPMG Sprocket’s sales operations. It highlights key metrics including total customers (2K), total revenue ($53K), total profit ($27K), and customer lifetime value (CLV Proxy: 153.68). The report analyzes revenue and profit trends by month, profit distribution by gender and brand, and state-level profitability. It also compares online versus offline sales performance, enabling clear visibility into customer behavior, geographic performance, and brand contribution to profitability.

Super Store Analysis Dashboard

This dashboard presents a high-level summary of overall business performance, featuring revenue (2.30M), total profit (286K), profit margin (12.47%), and total orders (38K). It includes category-level order distribution, regional profit analysis, sales representative performance, and team-based revenue and profit comparisons. Additionally, it provides customer segment insights (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office), enabling management to assess profitability drivers, team efficiency, and market segmentation trends effectively.
